  The two minor discrepancies
  - Target 1 df: only 103 participants rated both their own and the date's
    attractiveness. A paired test on 103 pairs has df = 102, so the paper's
    "t(103)" is probably the sample size reported in place of the df.
  - Target 6 confidence interval: the bootstrap interval changes with the random
    seed. I ran 10 seeds. The upper limit (0.02 to 0.04) covers the paper's
    0.04, but the lower limit (−0.64 to −0.58) never reached the paper's −0.57.
    The rest of the gap is probably a small difference between R's boot package
    and the authors' SPSS tool. Either way, the interval includes 0, which is
    why the paper calls this "marginal" mediation.
